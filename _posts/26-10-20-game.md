It's time again. I need to find out what I'm made of, again. Fill out forms, send resumes, ask nicely, implore aggressively, bargain with chips whose value my opponent decides, pull my nicest costumes out of the chests under my bed, cover my failures with lace and beads, blend in, stand out, and dance for my meal. Catch a fish and let it slip back over and over again, knowing that the next time I try, the fish will be even fewer, skinnier, and packed more densely with poison. Die slowly if I eat, starve slower if I don't. I'm done. I refuse to play your filthy game. I suspect that this is all a bluff to get me to put the noose around my own neck, and the only way to get out of this predicament is to keep staring the executioner in the eye until the gallows dissolve before my eyes and I find myself standing on the ground. I'll do what I would have done if I hadn't been shoved into a maze when I was a child, a maze with no exit, only the pretense that there are plenty of ways to a heavenly garden, and it's my fault that I haven't found them. I'll walk my own path. And if I don't lose my nerve, I might just walk straight through the walls.
